## What is Project Nobody?

Project Nobody is an open documentation movement.

It exists to prove that extraordinary things do not require extraordinary people.

They require ordinary people who choose to build consistently, document honestly, and improve together.

Project Nobody is not a startup.

Project Nobody is not a company.

Project Nobody is not a personal brand.

It is a public record of building.

---

# Why?

The internet has enough people telling others what to do.

Project Nobody is for people who actually build.

Every chapter...

Every experiment...

Every success...

Every mistake...

Every lesson...

...is documented publicly so anyone can learn from it.

---

# Our Mission

To document what becomes possible when ordinary people build together.

---

# Our Philosophy

We believe that...

- Building is better than consuming.
- Consistency beats talent.
- Documentation multiplies knowledge.
- Progress matters more than perfection.
- Communities are built by contribution, not attention.

---

# The Builder Mindset

A Builder is someone who chooses action over excuses.

Builders don't wait for permission.

Builders don't chase perfection.

Builders learn.

Builders share.

Builders improve.

Builders leave something behind.
